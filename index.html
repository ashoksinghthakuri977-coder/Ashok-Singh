About Me

Advocate Ashok Singh is licensed by the Nepal Bar Council. He has over four years of experience in legal practice, litigation, legal advisory, and legal research.

His professional interests include civil and criminal litigation, corporate and commercial law, company law, regulatory compliance, contract law, property and family law, international law, environmental and climate change law, and carbon market and carbon trading law.

He is committed to providing practical, research-driven and professional legal and advisory services.
